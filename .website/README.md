## Spectrum Sprites: PRISMSEED

PRISMSEED is a tiny procedural action roguelite made for js13k.

The idea was simple. Instead of trying to fit a whole game world into 13 KB, I used the space to build the rules that generate one.

A seed creates the regions, creatures, mutations, dungeons, Prism Wells, upgrades, and history of the world. You fight creatures, collect Prism XP, build up your character, restore all seven Wells, then return to the center and fight the Prism Devourer.

Beat it and the world loops with stronger enemies and more pressure.

There are generated creature types, mutation lineages, dungeons with keys and secrets, nine stackable upgrade genes, six build evolutions, elite enemies, persistent world events, procedural sound, and optional online co-op.

Online players using the same PrismSeed generate the same world locally. The game mostly sends player positions, damage, and important events instead of trying to send the whole world over the network.

You can also play completely solo.

### Controls

WASD / Arrow Keys: Move
Space / Left Click: Shoot
E: Interact
1 / 2 / 3 or Click: Pick an upgrade
Tab: Help
ONLINE: Connect or disconnect multiplayer
NEW: Wipe the current seed and start over

Death resets your current build back to level 1, but the world keeps its history. Restored Wells, mutations, loops, and other major events remain tied to that seed.
